Decision rule
Adopt simplified pathif paired correctness is non-inferior, safety stays perfect, p95/failure containment is no worse, and measured monthly TCO is lower at the observed consumer count.
Retain standardsif integration savings are realized by actual clients, tail reliability is better, or the projected consumer crossover falls inside the next 12-month committed roadmap.
Keep the experiment runningif intervals cross the non-inferiority margin, traffic lacks hard cases, or engineering logs are too sparse to price ownership honestly.
Creative falsifier: issue both implementations the same surprise change request—“add cancellation + resumable replay”—and include calendar time, defects, and migration work in TCO. If minimal code rapidly reconstructs AG-UI semantics, the standard may be cheaper after all.
